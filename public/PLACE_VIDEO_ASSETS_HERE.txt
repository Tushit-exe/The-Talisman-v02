The hero background currently defaults to the reference CloudFront video
from the original design spec, set directly in
src/components/ScrollVideo.tsx (VIDEO_SRC).

Whenever you have your own footage, you can either:
  - drop hero.mp4 (and optionally hero-poster.jpg) right here in /public,
    then change VIDEO_SRC in ScrollVideo.tsx to '/hero.mp4', or
  - just replace the CloudFront URL in VIDEO_SRC with a new hosted URL.

The page still works with nothing here (it falls back to a plain dark
background), so this folder is safe to leave empty in the meantime.
